Privacy Policy for "Worker: Word&Prayer"

Effective date: September 27, 2026 (first effective July 31, 2026)

Summary

This app does not operate a server. Everything you record — including prayer requests and reflection notes — is stored only on your device and never leaves it. The developer cannot access your records.

① Personal Information We Do Not Collect

The Service has no sign-up or login, and does not collect personally identifying information such as your name, email address, phone number, or account details.

② Data Stored on Your Device

The following data, which you create while using the Service, is stored only in local storage on your device and is never transmitted to the Provider's servers. The Provider cannot access this data.

ItemDetails
Prayer requests & answersTitles, memos, and answered status/date recorded in the prayer notebook
Reflection notes"My Reflection" text written per QT/Journey entry
Progress & streaksQT/Journey completion history, consecutive-day streak
App settingsNotification times, font size, sky background, language, onboarding status, etc.

This data is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can create your own backup file (JSON) using "Export data" in Settings; that file is saved only to a location you choose on your device and is never sent to the Provider.

③ A Note on Sensitive Content

Prayer requests and reflection notes may contain sensitive content, such as religious beliefs. This information is not collected, transmitted, or analyzed — it stays only on your device.

④ Payment Processing

Subscription payments are processed through the store the app was downloaded from. The Android app uses Google Play together with RevenueCat for payment processing; the iOS app uses Apple App Store in-app purchase. The iOS app does not use RevenueCat or any other third-party billing tool. In either case the app only checks whether a subscription is active; it does not store payment method details such as card numbers. Personal information processed by the store and by RevenueCat during payment is governed by their own privacy policies.

  • Google Play billing policy (Android): Google Play Help
  • Apple Privacy Policy (iOS): apple.com/legal/privacy
  • RevenueCat privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

⑤ Analytics

We do not use any analytics tool. The app may log the names of a small number of internal events (e.g., share-card use, viewing the subscription screen) locally on-device for product-quality purposes, but these never leave the device and never include any text you write (prayer requests, reflection notes, etc.).

⑥ Advertising

The Service does not display advertising and does not collect or use an Advertising ID.

⑦ Third-Party Sharing and International Transfer

The Provider does not share your personal information with third parties. Information that Google Play or RevenueCat (Android), or Apple (iOS), process independently during payment is outside the scope of this policy and is governed by their respective policies.

⑧ Your Rights

At any time, directly within the app, you can:

  • Edit or delete individual records such as prayer requests or reflection notes
  • Reset all records via "Start over from scratch" in Settings
  • Create a personal backup via "Export data" in Settings

Because the Provider does not hold a copy of your on-device data, you can delete all of it yourself using the methods above without needing to submit a separate deletion request.

⑨ Children Under 14

Because the Service does not collect personal information, it does not collect personal information from any user, including children under 14.

⑩ Security Measures

All user records are stored only in local storage on your device and are never transmitted externally. Since the Provider does not separately hold any user data, there is no risk of your records being exposed through a server breach.
